## 1. Voice-Logged Inspections

> Ref: [AI_ASSISTANT.md](AI_ASSISTANT.md) §2. Recording is queued and processed by a
> background worker (§2.1), not handled inline in the HTTP request. The worker only
> **proposes** actions (§2.2) — nothing is written to `inspections`/`treatments`/
> `harvests`/`feedings`/`hives` until the beekeeper explicitly Accepts a recording's
> proposals (§2.6); Reject discards them, nothing to undo either way.
